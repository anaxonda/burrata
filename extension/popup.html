Burrata

Open a Beatport, Traxsource, Shazam or Last.fm chart, a Spotify playlist or album, a Bandcamp album, or a Bandcamp Discover page.

The diagnostic saves one sanitized public track row to a local JSON file. Cookies, JSON scripts and forms are excluded. Nothing is uploaded.

Keep Burrata running. Spotify capture scrolls the page for up to 25 seconds and restores your position. Shazam and Last.fm chart captures preserve explicit ranks. Bandcamp Discover imports explicit tracks from up to 20 rendered album cards. Verification requires your interaction.
